Roadster Ramp

A drivable Three.js recreation of the ramp from Tesla's October 2026 Roadster reveal, with suction grip, cold-gas thrusters and rigid-body physics.

Built with

  • Agent: fx.sh
  • Model: Claude Opus 5.5
  • Rendering: Three.js

Inspiration & sources

  • Ramp: photos from the reveal at SpaceX McGregor, Texas
  • Fan suction & thrusters: Tesla, WIRED, The Verge, Engadget, Road & Track, TechRadar
  • Gas plume: based on React Bits, Flying Button
  • Roadster 3D model: based on this Sandbox game

Caveats

Ramp dimensions are estimated from photos. Mass, power, tyre grip and corner-thruster force are estimates; Tesla hasn't published them.
